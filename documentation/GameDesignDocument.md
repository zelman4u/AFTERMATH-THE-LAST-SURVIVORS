# AFTERMATH: The Last Survivors — Game Design Document

## 1. High Concept
**AFTERMATH: The Last Survivors** is a cross-platform 3D open-world zombie survival game playable in-browser across phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops, backed by an Express + Node.js API, Cloud Firestore, 14-collection document storage, and a Unity 6 C# client SDK.

## 2. Playable Survivor Roster (12 Characters)
- **Male Survivors (6)**: Ethan Walker (Scout), Daniel Reyes (Former Soldier), Marcus Vance (Heavy Engineer), Lucas Bennett (Bio-Chemist), Noah Reed (Mechanic), Gabriel Cruz (Hunter).
- **Female Survivors (6)**: Maria Santos (Combat Nurse), Elena Mercer (Sharpshooter), Sofia Delgado (Scavenger), Claire Moreau (Botanist), Maya Lin (Tactical Hacker), Rachel Thorne (Bunker Architect).

## 3. Open-World Regions (9 Zones)
1. **Safe Haven Outpost** — Starter camp with workbench, campfire, and perimeter palisade.
2. **Mercer Suburbs** — Residential homes, abandoned sedans, and basic supplies.
3. **Highway 9 Pileup** — Broken overpass asphalt, fuel caches, and runner ambushes.
4. **Oakwood Timber Forest** — Pine wilderness for timber, hunting, and shelter expansion.
5. **Central City Ruins** — High-rise rubble, medical clinics, and dense hordes.
6. **Ironworks Industrial Zone** — Corrugated steel factories and heavy workbench scrap.
7. **Fort Vanguard Military Base** — Heavy armor, assault rifles, and armored infected.
8. **Aegis Bio-Research Lab** — Antivirals, toxic spill hazards, and special mutants.
9. **Bunker Zero & Evacuation Port** — Endgame extraction zone guarded by World Bosses.

## 4. Survival & Evolution Systems
- **6 Core Meters**: Health, Hunger, Thirst, Stamina, Infection, Body Temperature.
- **24-Hour Day/Night Cycle & Weather**: Clear, Rain, Storm, Toxic Fog, Heatwave.
- **12 Infected & 4 World Bosses**: Walker, Runner, Crawler, Brute, Exploder, Spitter, Screamer, Bio-Armored, plus *The Behemoth*, *The Plague Queen*, *The Necromancer*, and *The Bio-Titan*.
- **Crafting, Base Building & Settlement Management**: 3-tier Workbench, fortified wooden/steel defenses, turrets, solar panels, farms, and recruitable NPC survivor tasks.
